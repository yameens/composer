educational tool to learn the theory behind chords, with your hands!

Camera (Mac): the app picks the built-in FaceTime camera and avoids an iPhone listed as “Continuity Camera.” If the wrong device opens, run with CONDUCTOR_CAM_INDEX=1 .venv/bin/python main.py (try 0, 1, 2). You can also turn off Continuity Camera in System Settings → General → AirDrop & Handoff.
